Implications for RO PlayZone / Tavern

What to design around next

✓ Design to the base
  • ▸
    Monetize with small, frequent buys — cheap cosmetic / deco gacha, not expensive whale packs
  • ▸
    Art direction for a female base — age 25–34, not teenage boys
  • ▸
    Reinforce owned-audience — the Thai base is still captured by a foreign platform (Y8) = the gap
⚠ Do not overlook
  • ▸
    Web/HTML5 earns ~97–99% from ads not IAP → the real question is who watches ads & retains
  • ▸
    Female casual base retains well good for ad revenue → fits the star / rewarded-ads design
  • ▸
    Don't expect a male-whale core like strategy / gacha — a different genre profile

Summary & sources

Short answer to which gender tops up more on web games:

Casual = women · all genres combined = men
  • ▸
    Player base across 3 portals: US leads Poki/Crazy · Thailand leads Y8 · Thai base 63% female, age 25–34
  • ▸
    Casual / puzzle / deco → women spend more, frequent small purchases · but on the web the main revenue is ads, not IAP
Sources: Similarweb · Semrush (Jun 2026) · MyTracker 2020 · Liftoff 2018 · CivicScience 2019 · Sensor Tower 2024 · Tenjin — gender/age are modeled estimates; per-bracket age % & per-gender spend for 2026 are not publicly available.
